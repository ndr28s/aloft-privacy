Aloft Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Aloft (비행멍) is a quiet ambient-sky app. This policy describes the data used by the current Android app and the optional Message Plane service.

Information Aloft uses

Aloft does not require a personal account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or advertising identifier.

  • Location: with permission, precise or approximate coordinates and device-reported altitude are used to render local time, weather, celestial objects, nearby aircraft, and a general place name. Fixed premium places can be used instead.
  • App settings: visual, sound, download, notification, premium-entitlement, Message Plane, and wallpaper preferences are stored on the device.
  • Message Plane identity: the app creates an app-scoped call sign and a random authentication secret. The relay stores the call sign and a one-way hash of the secret, not the secret itself.
  • Messages: a recipient call sign, sender call sign, message text, and timestamps are processed when you send a Message Plane. Current app input is limited to 12 Unicode code points without splitting a composed character; older relay clients support up to 30 UTF-16 code units.
  • Message safety: updated versions record your Message Plane terms version and acceptance time. The relay stores block relationships using app-scoped identity hashes, and a submitted report contains the received message, sender identity, reporter identity, reason, timestamps and review status. It does not use hardware identifiers to recognize a person.
  • Notifications: if enabled, an Android/Firebase Cloud Messaging token, platform, and language code are stored to deliver Message Plane alerts.

How location and network services are used

The device may send coordinates directly to MET Norway for weather, BigDataCloud for reverse geocoding, and adsb.lol for nearby live-aircraft data. These providers receive the network information normally included in an internet request and process it under their own policies. Live aircraft positions are not relayed through or stored by the Aloft Message Plane server.

Aloft may contact its own relay for Message Plane delivery, route enrichment from public datasets, push registration, and optional ambience or cloud-asset downloads. Retired internal flight-diagnostic collectors have been removed; the current app does not upload observation coordinates or flight-filtering samples to the relay.

Message retention

Message Plane text is transmitted over HTTPS but is not end-to-end encrypted. A pending message is kept only until it is claimed by the intended recipient or for a maximum of 24 hours, then deleted. Delivered and expired messages are removed during cleanup. Avoid sending personal, sensitive, illegal, or confidential information.

For the reporting feature, a separate delivery receipt containing the message and app-scoped sender/recipient identifiers is retained for up to 24 hours after receipt. If you report that message, the report and its review record are retained for up to 90 days after submission. A reviewed messaging restriction expires no later than that report. Cleanup runs once per minute while the relay is running and on startup.

The app keeps at most 20 recent received messages available for 24 hours so you can report or block their senders. Expired local entries are removed when the app next opens or accesses this history. Block relationships remain until you unblock the sender or request identity deletion; terms acceptance remains with the app-scoped relay identity.

Inactive push tokens are removed after up to 90 days and are also removed when Message Plane notifications or receiving are disabled where the device can reach the relay. The app-scoped call-sign record remains so the same local installation can authenticate and receive messages.

Photos and captures

Camera mode processes the camera preview on the device. Sky captures and camera composites are saved locally to the device's Pictures/Aloft collection when requested. Aloft does not upload those images to its relay.

Payments

Premium purchases and restoration are handled by Google Play. Aloft receives purchase and entitlement status needed to unlock features; it does not receive or store payment-card details.

Security

Aloft uses HTTPS for supported network requests, stores only a hash of the Message Plane authentication secret on the relay, restricts message length and rate, and lets users disable receiving and notifications. Updated versions also let recipients report a received message and block or unblock a sender. Blocking follows the same app identity across call-sign changes, but clearing app data or reinstalling can create a new identity. Notifications already delivered by the operating system may remain. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, and Message Plane should not be treated as a secure messenger.

Advertising, analytics, and children

Aloft currently contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK. Aloft is a general-audience ambient app and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request direct personal identifiers from children.

Your choices

In updated versions, use Settings > Receive messages to pause all incoming messages and message alerts. Turning it off removes pending messages; they will not be replayed when you turn it back on. Use Message safety in the message panel or Settings to report received messages and manage blocked senders. A report is disclosed to the Aloft operator for moderation; it does not automatically block the sender.

You may revoke location, camera, photo, or notification permission in Android settings; disable Message Plane receiving; remove the app and its local settings; or contact us about the relay identity associated with your Aloft call sign. Some features will be limited when permissions or network access are unavailable.

Request data deletion / 데이터 삭제 요청

To request deletion of your Aloft (비행멍) Message Plane data, email ndr28s@nate.com with the subject “Aloft data deletion” and your call sign if you still have it. If you no longer have the app or call sign, explain that in the email; reinstalling is not required to make a request. Do not send passwords, authentication secrets, payment-card details, or identity documents. We may ask for a minimal ownership check before deleting an identity, so another person cannot delete it using only its public call sign.

After confirming ownership, we remove the associated relay call-sign records, pending messages, reporting receipts, push-token registrations, preferences, consent and block records. A submitted abuse report or reviewed restriction may be retained for security and abuse prevention until its existing expiry, at most 90 days after the report. Local captures/settings can be removed through Android or by clearing app data. Google Play handles its own purchase records; deleting Aloft relay data does not delete those records or refund a purchase. Opening the app again may create/register a new installation identity.
