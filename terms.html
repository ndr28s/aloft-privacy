Reporting and controls

You can pause all receiving in Settings. Updated versions provide Message safety in the message panel and Settings: report a received message within 24 hours, block its sender, or unblock a sender. Blocking follows an app identity across call-sign changes, but is not a guarantee of recognizing the same person after they create a new installation identity. Already delivered system notifications may remain.

Reports are sent to the Aloft operator for review, with the message, app-scoped sender and reporter identities, reason and timestamps. Reports are kept for up to 90 days as described in the Privacy Policy. A report does not automatically restrict the sender or block them for you. Confirmed violations may result in removal of pending messages and a messaging restriction. For urgent concerns, a reporting-window expiry, or review of a restriction, contact ndr28s@nate.com.
